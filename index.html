About

Bridging AI Research & Industry

Wei-Jou (Edward) Duh is the Chief Executive Officer of the Artificial Intelligence Research Center (AINTU) at National Taiwan University. He holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Information Engineering from NTU, with a specialization in digital signal processing and multimedia systems.

Prior to his current role, Edward accumulated more than 20 years of experience in the fabless IC design industry, leading engineering teams through multimedia chip planning, design, validation, and turnkey solution development — with full P&L responsibility for product lines. His career spanned several successful startup cycles, including Oak Technology Inc. (NASDAQ: OAKT), Cheertek Inc. (TW: 3271), Qpixel Technology, Quanta Computer (TW: 2382), and Sigma Designs Inc. (NASDAQ: SIGM).

Since 2019, he has directed AINTU, coordinating over 30 AI research projects across multiple Taiwanese universities, managing large-scale NSTC programs covering core AI technology development and applications in healthcare and other sectors. His landmark Smart Emergency Capstone project demonstrated how AI can be defined, developed, and deployed in real hospital settings.

Edward has co-organized the annual AI Forum with Google for nine consecutive years, accumulating more than 90 Google speakers and 3,500 participants. He is deeply committed to closing the gap between academic research and industry needs.
